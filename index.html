VERIFY THE SOLVER

Test the homogeneous solution and resolution sensitivity at the same physical time, t = 2t_H, using N and 2N nodes. Agreement before strong collapse does not establish accuracy near the horizon; that regime needs a separate resolution study.

Numerical relativity solver · experimental prototype. Spherical symmetry, a perfect radiation fluid and smooth solutions before shocks or singularities. No AMR or shock-capturing. If the monitored local constraint residual exceeds 1%, the run is rejected without a physical verdict. Detecting a trapped sphere does not reconstruct a global event horizon.

01 / METRIC & MATTER

ds² = −A²dt² + B²dr² + R²dΩ²
p = ρ/3,   G = c = 1
U = A⁻¹∂ₜR,   Γ = B⁻¹R′
Γ² = 1 + U² − 2M/R
B = R′/Γ

Here t is the time of the exterior FLRW background, r labels comoving shells and R is the areal radius. A, B and R depend on t and r. The quantity ρ is the energy density in geometrized units.

02 / EVOLVED EQUATIONS

∂ₜR = AU
∂ₜU = −A[M/R² + 4πpR + Γ²p′/((ρ+p)R′)]
∂ₜM = −4πApUR²
∂ₜρ = −A(ρ+p)[2U/R + U′/R′]

Four fields evolve independently. Radial derivatives use a Chebyshev differentiation matrix. Time integration uses RK4, with the step limited by the acoustic characteristic speed and the local density-change rate.

03 / CONSTRAINTS & HORIZONS

M′ = 4πρR²R′
A′/A = −p′/(ρ+p)
θ₊ ∝ (U+Γ)/R
θ₋ ∝ (U−Γ)/R

The mass constraint is monitored independently rather than reimposed by integration after each step. The lapse follows analytically as A=(ρ_b/ρ)¹ᐟ⁴. A future-trapped sphere is flagged only when θ₊≤0 and θ₋<0.

Initial data, boundaries and numerical limitations

Gaussian curvature profile K(r) = (3eδ_m/2r_m²) exp[−(r/r_m)²]. Initially t_i=1, a_i=1, H_i=1/2 and r_m = 24 = 12 R_H(t_i), so ε_i = 1/12 and t_H=144. Initial data use the first nontrivial growing-mode long-wavelength approximation from SPriBHoS, rather than exact nonlinear constraint-solved data. The initial constraint residual is included in the diagnostics.

The domain extends to r_max=8r_m. At the centre R=U=M=0 and ρ′=0; at the outer boundary ρ′=0. The background is spatially flat FLRW with ρ_b=3/(32πt²). Residual outer-boundary effects require investigation before research use.

The local relative constraint residual is monitored between 0.2r_m and 2.5r_m, excluding the centre and outer boundary. It is not a complete solution-error estimate. Initial-data, spatial-discretization, time-integration and finite-domain errors remain.

The detector checks nodal signs every 10 steps and linearly interpolates the outer zero of U+Γ. The mass at first detection is particularly sensitive to resolution. There is no interior excision or evolution after detection. An interval ending without detection means only that no trapped sphere was detected by that time.
